## Labs

### Lab 02 – Basic NLP Preprocessing Techniques

In this lab, I practiced different methods for cleaning and preparing text before using it in NLP models.

I worked with tokenization, stopword removal, stemming, lemmatization, and text cleaning. I also compared NLTK and spaCy and tested different preprocessing configurations to see how they changed the original text.

**Main topics:** Tokenization, stopwords, stemming, lemmatization, and text preprocessing.

### Lab 04 – Text Representation

In this lab, I learned how computers convert text into numerical features that machine learning models can understand.

I worked with Bag of Words (BoW), TF-IDF, n-grams, cosine similarity, and word embeddings. I also trained text classification models using movie reviews and compared their performance.

In my classification experiment, Bag of Words achieved the highest accuracy of 63.3%, followed by n-grams at 60.0% and TF-IDF at 58.3%.

**Main topics:** BoW, TF-IDF, n-grams, word embeddings, document similarity, and text classification.

### Lab 05 – Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging

In this lab, I learned how NLP systems identify the grammatical roles of words in sentences.

I used NLTK and spaCy to identify nouns, verbs, adjectives, and other parts of speech. I compared tagging methods, tested ambiguous words, and explored challenges with informal language.

I also analyzed simulated customer service transcripts to identify important words and patterns that could help classify customer problems.

**Main topics:** POS tagging, NLTK, spaCy, word ambiguity, customer service text analysis, and error analysis.

## Midterm Project – NewsBot Intelligence System

For our midterm project, I worked with my teammates on the **NewsBot Intelligence System**, an NLP project that analyzes news articles.

**Team MAEL:** Matt Choo, Armani, Elham Timory, and Laura.

We used the BBC News Classification dataset to develop a system that processes news articles and performs several NLP tasks.

The project includes eight modules covering:

1. Text preprocessing
2. Part-of-speech tagging
3. Named entity recognition
4. Sentiment analysis
5. Text classification
6. Entity relationship analysis
7. Topic modeling
8. System integration and testing

We used machine learning models such as Logistic Regression, Multinomial Naive Bayes, and Random Forest to classify news articles into different categories.

In our initial model comparison, Multinomial Naive Bayes achieved the highest test accuracy at approximately 97.65%. We also tuned a Logistic Regression Pipeline, which was used in the final NewsBot demonstration.

For the final testing, we used three news articles from different categories to check how the system performed.
